# Lab Writeups

Walkthroughs of individual PortSwigger Academy labs.

PortSwigger labs are explicitly designed for public sharing — that's the whole point of the academy. So writeups here are fair game.

## Filename pattern

`<level>-<topic>-<short-slug>.md`

Examples:

- `apprentice-sqli-error-based-login-bypass.md`
- `practitioner-xss-csp-bypass.md`
